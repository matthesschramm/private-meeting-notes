# Travel policy

This policy applies to all business travel booked from 1 July 2026.

## Flights

Book economy class for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or more.
Business class needs approval from the chief operating officer before booking.

## Hotels and meals

Hotels must cost less than AUD 280 a night in Sydney and Melbourne, and less than AUD 220 a night elsewhere.
The daily meal allowance is AUD 95. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of returning, with receipts for anything over AUD 25.
