Office move budget memo

To: Office move steering committee
From: Finance

The board has approved a total budget of 320,000 dollars for the office move.

Conditions of the approval:
The contingency reserve must stay above 20,000 dollars at all times. If it falls below that, the committee must ask the board before committing more money.
Furniture contracts must include a warranty of at least five years.
Any lease extension on the current office longer than two months needs board approval.

Please report the committed spend to finance at the end of each month.
