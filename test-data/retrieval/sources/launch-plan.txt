Launch plan, version 3

Page 1: Overview

The product launches on 20 October. The press release goes out on the morning of launch day, subject to the final decision on timing.
Marketing will run a paid social campaign for two weeks after launch.

Page 2: Support

Support hours during launch week are 7 am to 10 pm, Melbourne time, seven days a week.
Two people from sales help with the chat queue, and one engineer joins support for the first three days.
The help centre publishes twelve new articles the day before launch.

Page 3: Risks

The main known risk is delayed background sync on older tablets when battery saver is on. The fix ships in the first patch release.
A second risk is the 25,000 dollar contingency for the launch event, which this plan does not cover.
